1 Introduction
An LLM agent’s performance on real tasks is shaped as
muchbytheharnessarounditsmodelasbythemodelitself:
the same frozen model, well- or poorly-harnessed, differs
sharply in what it accomplishes. By harness we mean the
fullruntimescaffolding,notonlythesystemprompttargeted

Khattab et al. 2024; Agrawal et al. 2026), but the injected

3.1 Problem setup: harness vs. kernel
WefixamodelM(weightsfrozen)anddefineanagentasM
wrapped in aharnessH, the deterministic scaffolding that
turnsMinto a task-solving system: the system/role prompt,
injected knowledge and tool definitions, the control loop (it-

Self-evolution searches overHwithMheld constant.
Not all ofHis mutable. We partition it into akernel
that evolution must not touch (measurement and evaluation
