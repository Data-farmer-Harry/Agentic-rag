H. Additional Related Work
Predicting success from internal representations.
Frozen-activation probes decode knowledge, truth, and hal-
lucinationsignalsthatmodelsdonotreliablyverbalize(Alain
and Bengio 2016; Belinkov 2022; Kadavath et al. 2022;
Azaria and Mitchell 2023; Burns et al. 2023; Marks and
Tegmark2024;Orgadetal.2025;Kossenetal.2024;Jietal.
2024). Internal states also anticipate chain-of-thought suc-
cess and encode self-verification (Afzal et al. 2025; Zhang
et al. 2025). In agent settings, Padhi et al. (2026) recover
success/failure directions for representation steering, while
MEMPROBE targets persistent memory rather than online
residualactivations(Maetal.2026),andMehta(2026)study
commitment,deliberatelyseparatedfromcorrectness.These
results establish that outcome-relevant signal exists in the
residual stream and is linearly accessible; we ask when it
becomesactionableandturnitintoastoppingrulewithcon-
trolled error.
FailuredetectionandmonitoringofLLMagents.Deci-
sive agent errors often arise early and compound (Zhu et al.
2025; Li et al. 2026); AgentRx localizes such steps post hoc
(Barke et al. 2026). Online approaches range from text or
embedding auditors, including AgentForesight, weakly su-
pervisedalerters,andMASC(Zhangetal.2026;Baidyaetal.
2026; Shen et al. 2026), to behavioral statistics and low-cost
runtime signals (Chen et al. 2026; Pham et al. 2026). Richer
monitors use diagnostic agents, typed events, or execution
graphs (Mittapalli et al. 2026; Dellibarda Varela et al. 2026;
Huang et al. 2026; Zhao 2026); other methods alter refusal,
stop on repetition, or elicit self-reports (Guan et al. 2026;
Shrivastava 2026; Lin et al. 2026; Lu et al. 2025). Our acti-
vation reader needs no auxiliary LLM pass, and its cascade
controls retained-success recall over all checks.
Conformal prediction and risk control for LLMs.Split
conformal and related calibration give finite-sample guaran-
tees under exchangeability (Vovk, Gammerman, and Shafer
2005; Papadopoulos et al. 2002; Lei et al. 2018; An-
gelopoulos and Bates 2023), with extensions to general
risks (Bates et al. 2021; Angelopoulos et al. 2025, 2024).
LLM applications include prediction sets, factuality filters,
and help-seeking planners (Quach et al. 2024; Mohri and
Hashimoto 2024; Cherian, Gibbs, and Candès 2024; Ren
et al. 2023). Davidov et al. (2026) derive optimal quitting
from a learned value function. CALM composes token ex-
its under a sequence-level constraint (Schuster et al. 2022),
while FIPER calibrates robot-policy alarms from successful
rollouts (Römer et al. 2025). Our fixed-policy certificate in-
steadcontrolsretained-successrecallacrossanepisode-level
sequence of gates.
